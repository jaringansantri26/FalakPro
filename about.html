Tentang Aplikasi FalakPro

FalakPro adalah aplikasi astronomi Islam dan ilmu falak profesional yang dikembangkan oleh Lembaga Falakiyah Pengurus Wilayah Nahdlatul Ulama (PWNU) Jawa Barat.

Aplikasi ini dirancang sebagai platform terpadu berakurasi tinggi untuk memenuhi kebutuhan hisab, rukyat, kalender astronomis, penentuan arah kiblat, waktu salat, gerhana, hingga pengamatan hilal modern bagi kalangan pesantren, akademisi, lembaga hisab rukyat, kementerian agama, peneliti, dan umat Islam luas.

FalakPro memadukan Astronomi Modern Komputasional (teori VSOP87D, ELP/MPP02, dan Jean Meeus Astronomical Algorithms) dengan khazanah Hisab Turats Klasik (seperti metode kitab Ad-Durr al-Aniq fi Ilm al-Falak dan Sullamun Nayyirain) dalam satu antarmuka yang elegan, cepat, dan 100% offline.

Fitur Unggulan FalakPro

🌙 Hisab Awal Bulan & Peta Hilal

Perhitungan komprehensif Ijtimak, tinggi hilal mar'i/hakiki, elongasi, umur, fraksi iluminasi, lebar sabit, dan peta visibilitas global interaktif (MABIMS 3°/6.4°, Wujudul Hilal, Odeh, Yallop, Danjon, Turki/KGHT).

📷 Kamera Rukyat Hilal (AR)

Pengamatan hilal berbantu Augmented Reality, pengunci posisi sensor (*Lock Sensor*), penggaris koordinat derajat langit, dan pelacak matahari/bulan real-time.

🧭 Kompas & Kamera Kiblat Presisi

Penentuan arah kiblat berbasis rumus Great Circle, Ellipsoid WGS84, dan Vincenty Formula dilengkapi fitur kamera AR serta Rashdul Qiblah harian & tahunan.

🕌 Jadwal Salat & Imsakiyah

Jadwal salat akurat dengan koreksi elevasi pengamat, berbagai kriteria hisab internasional (LFNU, Kemenag, MWL, ISNA, Ummul Qura), serta ekspor PDF A4 siap cetak.

🗓️ Kalender Terpadu 4-in-1

Sinkronisasi kalender Masehi, Hijriyah, Saka Jawa (lengkap dengan 5 Pasaran), dan Caka Sunda (Pancawara, Sadwara, dan Wuku).

☀️🌕 Gerhana Matahari & Bulan

Kalkulasi presisi waktu kontak gerhana (U1, U2, Puncak, U3, U4), magnitudo, tipologi gerhana, dan data geometri bayangan umbra/penumbra.

📊 Data Falak & Ephemeris

Tabel ephemeris harian/jam-jaman Matahari & Bulan (RA, Deklinasi, GHA, LHA, EoT, Obliquity, GAST, Delta T) dengan ekspor spreadsheet Excel (.xlsx).

🧮 Kalkulator Ilmiah Falak

Kalkulator trigonometri astronomis dengan input pecahan derajat-menit-detik (DMS) dan konversi waktu astronomis.

About FalakPro

FalakPro is a premier Islamic astronomy, calendrical computation, and celestial observation suite developed by Lembaga Falakiyah PWNU West Java (Indonesia).

Engineered for Islamic scholars, astronomers, academic institutions, mosques, and enthusiasts worldwide, FalakPro provides a unified and offline-first platform for precision ephemeris generation, Hijri calendar reckoning, Qibla determination, eclipse prediction, and augmented reality crescent sighting.

The application uniquely synthesizes Modern High-Precision Astrophysics (VSOP87D solar theory, ELP/MPP02 lunar theory, IAU 2000A nutation, and Jean Meeus algorithms) with revered Classical Islamic Astronomical Traditions (such as Ad-Durr al-Aniq and Sullamun Nayyirain).

⚡

Core Features

🌙 Hijri Crescent & Visibility Maps

Instant computation of Conjunction, Topocentric/Geocentric Crescent Altitude, Elongation, Crescent Width, Moon Age, Illumination, and interactive global visibility zones (MABIMS, Wujudul Hilal, Odeh, Yallop, Danjon, Turkey/KGHT).

📷 AR Crescent Rukyat Camera

Augmented Reality horizon alignment, real-time Sun and Moon tracking, 3D celestial coordinate ruler grid, and sensor orientation locking for telescope mounting.

🧭 Precision Qibla Finder

Multi-model Qibla calculation (Spherical, WGS84 Ellipsoid, and Vincenty geodesic formula) with live camera AR guidance and daily solar shadow (Rashdul Qiblah) data.

🕌 Prayer Times & Imsakiyah

Accurate prayer times adjusted for observer elevation and dip of horizon, configurable international conventions (LFNU, Kemenag, MWL, ISNA, Ummul Qura), and A4 PDF printing.

🗓️ Unified 4-in-1 Calendar

Seamless synchronization between Gregorian, Hijri, Javanese Saka (Pasaran cycles), and Sundanese Caka systems.

☀️🌕 Solar & Lunar Eclipses

Rigorous contact timing (U1, U2, Max, U3, U4), magnitude, typology, and umbral/penumbral shadow geometry for any global coordinate.

📊 Full Astronomical Ephemeris

Daily and hourly astronomical ephemeris for the Sun and Moon with one-click export to formatted Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx).

🧮 Astronomical Calculator

DMS angle conversion, fractional time calculations, and specialized trigonometric functions.

عن تطبيق فلك برو (FalakPro)

فلك برو (FalakPro) هو تطبيق رائد ومتخصص في علم الفلك الإسلامي والحسابات الفلكية ورصد الأهلة، تم تطويره بواسطة مؤسسة الفلكية التابعة لـ PWNU جاوة الغربية، إندونيسيا.

صُمم التطبيق ليكون منصة متكاملة عالية الدقة تلبي احتياجات المعاهد الإسلامية والعلماء والباحثين والمؤسسات الدينية في حساب التقاويم وتحديد مواقيت الصلاة واتجاه القبلة والتنبؤ بالكسوف والخسوف ورصد الهلال.

يجمع التطبيق بين أحدث النظريات الفلكية العالمية (مثل VSOP87D و ELP/MPP02 ونظريات جان ميوس) وكتب الفلك الإسلامي التراثية المعتمدة (مثل كتاب "الدر الأنيق في علم الفلك" وكتاب "سلم النيرين").
